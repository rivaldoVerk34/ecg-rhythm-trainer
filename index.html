Three things most ECG apps do not do together

Practice

Name the rhythm

Short exercises on real recorded traces from open research databases. Pick the rhythm, get the answer straight away.

Understand

See the evidence

The correction draws on the trace: beat intervals, missing P waves, wide QRS complexes. You learn what to look at, not just what to answer.

Adapt

Train your weak pairs

The app keeps a map of the rhythms you confuse, for example flutter taken for fibrillation, and serves those next.

Built to be read in your language

A rhythm looks the same in Lagos, Lyon, Lima and Lahore. Only the explanation changes, so the app launches in many languages at once. Each language is marked as reviewed by a health professional, or as beta until one has checked it.
